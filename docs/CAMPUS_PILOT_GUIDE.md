# Swiggy Campus Circles — Campus Pilot Guide

> **Audience:** Campus Ambassadors & Circle Organizers at PW Institute of Innovation Noida.

---

## 1. Getting Started
1.  **Launch a Circle:** Gather your squad (Hostel roommates, lab partners, or hackathon teams).
2.  **Define Preferences:** Set your individual vegetarian preferences and maximum lunch budget in Settings.
3.  **Invite Members:** Share your unique Circle Code (e.g., `CSE-91`).

---

## 2. Running a Group Order
-   **Schedule Sessions:** Use "Recurring Meal Windows" to automate lunch sessions at 1:00 PM daily.
-   **Collaborative Selection:** Ensure all members have added their dishes before the 12:30 PM cutoff.
-   **Finalization:** The Circle Admin must click "Finalize Order" to lock the cart.

---

## 3. Pickup Coordination
-   **Designated Drop-off:** Meet at the PWII Noida Hostel Gate 1.
-   **The Organizer Dashboard:** The student who placed the order uses the "Organizer Collection Dashboard" in the Pickup Pass tab.
-   **QR Collection:** Scan each student's Pickup QR to mark their individual meal as collected. This prevents confusion in large 10+ member orders.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting
-   **Out-of-Stock Dishes:** If the restaurant calls about a missing item, trigger the "Order Rescue AI" to find a compatible replacement quickly.
-   **Payment Settling:** Use the "CirclePay Tracker" to ensure every member has reimbursed the organizer. Mark as "Confirmed" only after verifying the transaction on your UPI app.

---

## 5. Feedback & Reporting
Report any bugs or feature requests to the development team at the Swiggy Hackathon GitHub repository issues page.
