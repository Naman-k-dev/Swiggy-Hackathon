# Swiggy Campus Circles — Judge Demo Script (3-Minute Tour)

> **Goal:** Showcase the end-to-end student lifecycle from Circle creation to collective pickup.

---

## Preparation (0:00 - 0:15)
1.  **Login:** Open the app and log in using a demo persona (e.g., "Naman" - Circle Admin).
2.  **Home Screen:** Show the student dashboard with "Upcoming Lunch Squad" session.

---

## Phase 1: Collective Decision Making (0:15 - 1:00)
1.  **Circle View:** Navigate to "CSE Lunch Squad".
2.  **CircleMatch AI:** Click "Run CircleMatch AI". Explain how it finds one restaurant (e.g., "Mainland China") that fits everyone's budget (₹200-₹500) and dietary needs.
3.  **CircleVote:** Open a restaurant vote. Simulate Student B and C voting in real-time. Finalize the winning restaurant.

---

## Phase 2: Collaborative Cart & Order Rescue (1:00 - 2:00)
1.  **Shared Cart:** Add "Veg Hakka Noodles" to your cart. Show other members adding items.
2.  **Budget Guardrails:** Try to add a ₹1000 dish. Watch the budget warning appear.
3.  **Order Rescue AI:** Simulate "Out of Stock" for a member's item. Show the Order Rescue AI suggesting a "Schezwan Fried Rice" replacement that respects the member's budget and dietary preferences. Accept the rescue.

---

## Phase 3: Checkout & Post-Order Coordination (2:00 - 3:00)
1.  **Simulated Checkout:** Confirm the group order. Show the "Group Deal" discount applied for reaching the ₹1000 threshold.
2.  **CirclePay Tracker:** Show the settlement dashboard. Mark your share as "Sent". Explain how this tracks internal group dues without collecting real payments.
3.  **Pickup Pass:** Open the QR code dashboard. Demonstrate the per-meal collection checklist used at the hostel gate drop-point.

---

## Bonus: Campus Insights (Optional)
-   **Campus Demand Radar:** Open the analytics tab to show how the system forecasts peak hostel demand on Friday nights based on historical group patterns.
